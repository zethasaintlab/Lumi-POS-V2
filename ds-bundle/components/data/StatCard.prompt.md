Kartu KPI dasbor/reservasi. `tone` mewarnai garis-atas untuk identitas modul (bukan aksi utama). Angka tabular.

```jsx
<StatCard label="Today" value="0" tone="violet" icon={<Icon name="calendar" size={16} />} hint="Bookings for today" />
<StatCard label="Penjualan neto" value="Rp 3.501.000" tone="accent" delta="12% vs kemarin" deltaDir="up" />
```
