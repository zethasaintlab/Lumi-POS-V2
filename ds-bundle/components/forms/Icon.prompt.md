Ikon stroke SVG gaya Lucide (viewBox 24, stroke-width 2) yang mewarisi `currentColor`; pakai di dalam tombol, badge, dan input. Hanya glyph yang dibutuhkan POS.

```jsx
<Icon name="alert" size={14} />
<button className="btn btn-primary"><Icon name="check" size={18} /> Siap</button>
```

Glyph tersedia: search, chevron-down/left/right, alert, check, x, plus, minus, wifi-off, refresh, clock, receipt, lock. Ukuran default 20px; badge biasanya 14px, tombol 18px.
